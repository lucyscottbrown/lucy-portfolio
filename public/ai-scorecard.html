Public-Sector AI Use-Case Scorecard

Describe an AI use case for a public-sector organisation and score it on five factors. The tool calculates a priority score, plots it against value and feasibility, suggests the metrics and test data needed to evaluate it, and lists the checks it should pass before going live. The checks get stricter as the risk score goes up.
