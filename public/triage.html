CAR INSURANCE CLAIMS · WORKING PROTOTYPE

Triage — an adjuster's copilot for damage claims

Reads the damage, drafts the estimate, and states exactly how sure it is — clearing the claims it should clear on its own, and handing everything else to a human adjuster with the evidence already attached. Not a chatbot, not an autopilot: a risk-scaled decision made explicit, with the dials exposed rather than buried in code.

Repair estimate above this share of vehicle value → write-off review, never an automatic repair.

These four numbers are illustrative starting points, not validated production values — the point of exposing them as dials instead of burying them in code is to force this conversation with the insurer explicitly, before launch, rather than discover the right thresholds by incident. They apply globally — try moving one, then switch claims above. The escalate floor is kept at least 5 points below the auto-approve bar on purpose — let them cross and auto-approve goes quietly unreachable with no warning.
